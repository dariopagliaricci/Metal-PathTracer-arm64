# Metal PathTracer v4.1.0

This release covers the work done since v4.0.0 (PRs #144 to #191 in the
private line). It has two themes: the Embree CPU backend follows Metal on more
cases, and several shadow-ray, importer and loader rules were fixed and
measured. Most images do not change. The ones that do are listed in the
upgrade notes.

Development happens in a private line, and each release is imported into this
repository as a single snapshot commit.

## Source

- Repository: https://github.com/dariopagliaricci/Metal-PathTracer-arm64
- Tag: `v4.1.0`

## Build

The build commands are those of v4.0.0. Basic Metal build:

```bash
cmake -S . -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release
cmake --build build --target PathTracer PathTracerHeadless
```

Embree-enabled build:

```bash
brew install embree
cmake -S . -B build-embree -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DPATH_TRACER_ENABLE_EMBREE=ON
cmake --build build-embree --clean-first --target PathTracerHeadless EmbreeSmokeTest
```

The importer, OIDN and the CMake options are as in the v4.0.0 notes. Scene
assets are now linked into the build directories instead of copied (see the
upgrade notes).

## Highlights

- The Embree backend runs Metal's homogeneous media, absorbing and scattering.
  Sun plane with sigma_a 0.5: 2.33004 to 1.81348, the exact value. `rect_big`,
  single scattering: 0.05902 against the reference 0.059010.
- The Embree backend runs Metal's hero-wavelength spectral transport, with the
  same refusals. `living_room` PASS 0.9999 (50/50 tiles).
- The Embree backend runs Metal's plain RIS in every RIS mode. `living_room`
  `ris`, Embree/Metal: 0.9942 to 1.0007. The reuse of `ris_spatial`,
  `ris_temporal`, `ris_world` and `ris_regir` is not ported; those modes render
  plain RIS on Embree.
- Embree follows Metal's source opacity and `interfacePrecision` rules without
  source transport. `bedroom` Embree/Metal: 0.8524 to 1.0002 (120/120 tiles).
  It also matches Metal on mixed nearest/linear texture filters, anisotropic
  texture levels, normal maps on zero vertex tangents and shadow-ray media.
- Wavefront shadow kernels no longer let light through blockers. An analytic
  rectangle blocker at scale 10 read 1.2735 on the hardware wavefront and now
  reads 1.0000. The software tracer now sees analytic spheres in front of
  triangles (sphere at scale 10, SW/HW: 1.1010 to 1.0000).
- Headless Metal renders can resume from sample checkpoints
  (`--checkpointEverySamples=N`, `--resumeCheckpointJson`). Resumed renders are
  byte-identical to uninterrupted ones on the sponza cases tested.
- The importer skips FBX Models hidden in the source file, and applies Cinema
  4D polygon selections. On `zero_day_measure_seven`, full depth, Embree/Metal HW
  is 1.0009 and SW/HW is 0.9999 after both. Both rules change Zero Day images;
  the packs are local-only.
- Builds link the scene assets instead of copying about 53 GB per destination.
- Power-of-two 8-bit colour textures use the shared mip chain on Metal and
  Embree. `bistro_full_sun_restir_alt` Embree/Metal: 0.9953 to 0.9998.
- Software ray tracing resumes 5 mm past an excluded spawn triangle, as hardware
  does. `zero_day_measure_one` SW/HW, megakernel: 0.9917 to 0.9983.
- `ris_spatial` runs its neighbour reuse on the wavefront instead of plain RIS.
  `living_room` direct light against plain NEE: 0.9997.
- Embree's anisotropic filter follows Metal's sampler. Zero Day Seven floor tile
  (384,192): 1.1192 to 1.0022.
- Coated random-walk subsurface materials are layered as in Arnold, on Metal
  and Embree, and a `type=sss` material takes a normal map that shapes its
  coat. On the matched sphere the coat-on frame went from 0.802 to 0.9994 of
  Arnold. This changes the look of two shipped presets: `sss_marble_wax` +7.2%
  and `stanford-dragon` +3.7%.
- Embree loads big scenes faster: its material textures copy on six threads.
  `emerald_square_day` load 13.1-13.9 s to 4.6-5.1 s; peak footprint rises by
  0.6-1.1 GB.
- The headless megakernel and wavefront have lighter default-features pipelines.
  Megakernel time against the same build before #182, 1280x720, 32 spp: `sponza` 0.926,
  `sun_temple` 0.962. Wavefront: `emerald_square_day` 0.961, `sponza` 0.912.
- Peak load memory fell with no image change. `barcelona_pavilion_day`, Metal:
  16.46 GB to 9.17 GB before the packed-data fix, then 5.43 GB. Embree:
  13.70 GB to 6.44 GB.

## Changes in maturity

Only the rows that change from v4.0.0.

| System | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Embree CPU reference backend | Runs Metal's homogeneous media with scattering, hero-wavelength spectral transport, plain RIS in the RIS modes and source opacity without transport. It no longer ignores volumes. |
| Hero-wavelength spectral transport | Runs on Metal and Embree. Path-space caustics stay Metal only. |
| Headless sample checkpoints (Metal) | New. Refused by name with state-keeping modes, Embree and runs without `--output`. |

## Known limits

- Embree's reuse in `ris_spatial`, `ris_temporal`, `ris_world` and `ris_regir`
  is not ported. Those modes render plain RIS and Embree logs a warning.
- Embree path-space caustics are ignored, in RGB and in spectral mode.
- A `type=sss` normal map shapes only the coat. It is not applied at the walk's
  exit, and with the coat off or `method=separable` it does nothing.
  `method=separable` is not implemented.
- On the stripes normal-map probe, Metal is 0.9815 and 0.9895 of its no-map
  frame at mean free paths 0.1 and 0.25, against Arnold's 0.998 and 1.0024.
  The cause of this 1.3-1.7% gap is not known.
- `type=plastic` keeps Schlick's Fresnel. Only coated random-walk subsurface
  uses the exact dielectric Fresnel.
- The wavefront's default-features pipelines change images in their last bits
  (about 0.2% of pixels take another path); frame means do not move.
- Sample checkpoints are refused with `restir_di` and the other direct-light
  modes except `legacy`, `baseline_emissive` and `ris`; with SVGF, ReSTIR GI,
  ReSTIR PT, path guiding, the radiance cache, caustic transport and the ML
  backend; with `--validate-ris` and Embree; and without `--output`. A completed
  manifest from a run without `--checkpointEverySamples` still renders the frame
  again.
- Zero Day: after the importer fixes, SW is darker than HW and Embree on one
  canister in Measure Seven (tile SW/HW 0.655), and on Measure One HW is about
  0.7% above SW and Embree. The Zero Day floor difference on Embree is not
  closed: the full-depth tiles at (224,192) and (384,192) did not move with the
  anisotropic fix (#158).
- Embree source shadow rays under opacity keep a 1.6-2% residual under a glass
  slab, also with a legacy boundary. It is left for a follow-up (#164).
- Embree still refuses by name: alpha cutouts on explicit-interior materials
  under source transport (no input reaches it).
- Zero Day's installed packs keep their Nov 19 KTX2 files and calibrations. A
  fresh import differs in 223 of 356 `measure_one` KTX2 files (#171).

## Upgrade notes

- **Zero Day imports.** The importer skips FBX Models that the file hides, and
  drops the triangles that a Cinema 4D polygon selection covers on its base
  mesh. A re-import of the Zero Day FBX files changes the images: with the
  hidden-model skip, the Metal HW frame mean at 960x540 moved by x1.0232
  (`zero_day_measure_one`) and x1.0752 (`zero_day_measure_seven`); the polygon
  selection rule moved Measure Seven by x0.9877 and Measure One by x1.0000. The
  calibrations were fitted on the old import. ASCII FBX files are not read.
- **Barcelona Pavilion loader rules.** The glTF loader selects them with the
  mesh token `gltfBarcelonaPavilionRules=1`, not the file path. A copy of the
  asset under another name needs the token. Without it the loader logs a
  warning, and the image differs (80.9% of pixels on `barcelona_pavilion_day`,
  mean error 0.176). The three Pavilion presets carry the token.
- **Mirrored glTF primitives.** A normal-mapped primitive with no TANGENT
  accessor under a mirrored node had a flipped bitangent. It is fixed. No
  shipped preset has such a primitive.
- **Zero vertex tangents.** A normal map on an all-zero vertex tangent now uses
  the UV frame on Metal and Embree. With degenerate UVs it is not applied.
  Only Zero Day has such tangents; its images did not change measurably.
- **Software ray tracing.** Analytic spheres in front of triangles are now
  visible, and wavefront shadow rays are no longer lit through blockers on
  both ray-tracing paths. Scenes that mix spheres or rectangles with meshes may
  change. The 8 shipped scenes checked that mix rectangles with meshes were
  identical.
- **Embree volumes.** Embree no longer ignores `--volumeTransportMode=homogeneous`.
  Embree renders of media change from the unattenuated image.
- **Embree spectral mode.** `--spectralMode=hero_wavelength` now runs on Embree.
  It refuses the same combinations as Metal, with exit code 2.
- **Embree RIS modes.** `ris` and the reuse modes now render Metal's plain RIS
  on Embree, not `legacy`.
- **Power-of-two colour mips.** Presets with power-of-two 8-bit colour textures
  change on Metal. The change is under 1% in the frame mean on the 17 sweep
  presets (lowest: `bistro_full_sun_restir_alt` 0.9955).
- **Zero Day emitters.** Metal weights textured emitters by linear power since
  the BC sRGB decode fix. Zero Day images change within noise in the frame mean;
  per-pixel noise is 1.2-1.7% lower.
- **Software ray tracing resume.** Software-traced images change slightly
  (`swrt_sponza` differs on 0.2% of pixels). Hardware images do not change.
- **Build directories.** `assets/` is symlinked into each build directory and
  the app bundle. An older real copy of `assets/` is left in place with a
  warning. Move it to the Trash once, and the next build links. The app bundle
  is no longer self-contained: a `PathTracer.app` copied out of its build tree
  does not find the bundled scenes. Run it from the build directory.
- **ACEScg.** The working colour space `ACEScg` is removed. No setting could
  select it.
- **Coated subsurface looks.** `sss_marble_wax` is 7.2% brighter and
  `stanford-dragon` 3.7% brighter, wavefront HW, 960x540, 256 spp (frame mean
  0.30115 to 0.32285 and 0.66757 to 0.69255). This is a deliberate look change,
  from the Arnold layering of coated random-walk subsurface materials (#186,
  #191). The owner accepted it. Coat-off subsurface materials do not change,
  and neither does the separable wax bust.
- **Subsurface normal maps.** A `type=sss` material takes `normalTexture=` and
  `normalScale=`. The map shapes only the coat (#188, #189).
- **OBJ and PLY normal maps.** A normal map on an OBJ or PLY mesh uses the UV
  frame, not world +x (#187). No shipped preset reads it.
- **Wavefront images.** The wavefront shade kernel's default-features pipeline
  changes pixels in their last bits in headless runs (#185). Frame means are
  1.00000 on five scenes.
- **Checkpoint files.** A render with `--checkpointEverySamples` now keeps its
  `<output>.accum` after it completes. It is the accumulation textures at the
  render size.

## Verification

Each CHANGELOG number was measured on a scene or an analytic test, against an
exact value, the Embree backend or Metal, as its PR states. For Metal-affecting
changes the 18-row Metal gate (the v4.0.0 freeze rows) was 18/18 IDENTICAL in
the PRs that state it (#147, #150, #151, #156, #157, #159, #160, #162-#168,
#170, #173, #176, #182-#184, #186-#188). #185 changes the wavefront in its
last bits and is described in the CHANGELOG. The two importer PRs (#161, #169) change images on purpose, were
measured against the Embree and software backends, and were merged on Dario's
approval after he reviewed their panels. This release makes no speed
claim beyond the timings listed in the CHANGELOG.

### Image changes against v4.0.0

The release-candidate probe rendered both v4.1.0 and v4.0.0 from the same
local assets:
- the 34 Metal lines of the v4.0.0 rc2 probe, at 1/4 resolution, one EXR each,
  seed 42;
- the two Embree release images, 1920x1080 at 256 spp.

A Metal line counts as changed when its frame mean moves by more than 0.55%, or
one tile by more than 28.4%. That is three times the seed-to-seed noise
measured on three of the lines.

- **Metal.** One line changes beyond that threshold: `sss_marble_wax`, frame
  mean 1.0721 of v4.0.0 (largest tile 24.5%), from the Arnold layering of
  coated random-walk subsurface materials (#186). This is the accepted look
  change in the upgrade notes. `stanford-dragon` (+3.7%) has no line in the
  probe. Below the threshold, three PRs move some lines, and the probe found
  each move at a merge point:
  - #172 (power-of-two colour mips) makes indirect light slightly darker where
    bounces read coarse mips. Bistro sun is -0.46%, sponza sunlight -0.20% and
    Bistro night -0.10%. The F1 cars and the Bugatti move by -0.01% to -0.02%.
  - #174 (BC sRGB emitter power) changes which emitter next-event estimation
    picks. Frame means stay within 0.01% (Emerald day 0.9999, Emerald dusk and
    Zero Day Seven 1.0000). Tiles move at noise level, at most 2.29% on
    Emerald dusk.
  - #180 (re-spawn frame) moves hardware lines at noise level only: tiles at
    most 0.76% on `gltf-damaged-helmet` and 0.07% on `sun_temple`, frame means
    1.0000. It was bisected to one commit on those two lines.
  - Every other line keeps its frame mean within noise. Small tile moves on
    some lines were not bisected; they fit #180 and, on two wavefront lines,
    #185's float order. Both Pavilion views and the Bitterli wavefront scenes
    are among them.
- **Embree.** The release images have frame means of 0.9998 (sponza
  sunlight) and 1.0001 (Bitterli staircase) of v4.0.0's, denoised and raw.
  Both are byte-identical to the same scenes rendered before #184, #189 and
  #191. Those PRs do not reach these scenes: neither has a `type=sss`
  material.

The probe does not cover asset changes made since v4.0.0: the Zero Day
re-imports (#161, #169) and the Barcelona Pavilion preset edit in the asset
pack:
- The re-imports were measured in their own PRs.
- The preset edit removes an inert `causticTransportMode=path_space` token. It
  gives the same image (frame mean 1.0000 on all three views). The megakernel
  runs 1.8x to 2.2x faster at 1280x720.
